## Question

On the 2060 (PCIe 3.0), is the 7-8 % loss of the sparse default at 64k (P123, P127) the shorter VRAM line or a cost
inside the engine?

## Why

The 4070's D26a (PCIe 4.0): engine overhead 0 (+0.2 %), line cost 2.6 %. On the 2060, P123 and P127 lost 7-8 %, and the
loss did not scale with the reserved cells. D26 (sparse without a line cost) needs to know which part to remove.

## Plan and gate

Script header: "Reading (declared before the run): on_short vs off_short = engine overhead; off_long vs off_short = the
line cost."

## Method

Three arms alternated x2 (off_short, on_short, off_long per round), one server each, quick_tps 64000.

## Results

| arm | line | r1 | r2 | sparse lines |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| off_short | 52,224 (pinned) | 35.4 | 35.4 | 0 |
| on_short | 52,224 | 35.5 | 35.4 | 0 |
| off_long | 61,696 | 36.4 | 36.3 | 0 |

0 one-token in all six arms.

Notebook: "D26a on Turing - no engine overhead (+0.1 %); line cost -2.6 % (52,224 vs 61,696) = the 4070's."

Reading (notebook): "P123 / P127's -7 / -8 % came from off arms at 61.7k-63.5k serving 64k with ~no host tail: the 64k
cost on Turing is mostly a step (any host tail vs none), small slope after. For D26: bounds must not take line from
the no-tail range (staging span idea), a smaller reserve alone does not help."

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- None. All arms ran 0 sparse ops at 64k: the host tail is below MIN_HOST (32,768 cells) in every arm (at most
  ~11.8k cells past the 52,224 line).

## Verdict

DONE. On Turing the sparse fixed cost at 64k is the VRAM line, not the engine (+0.1 %). The 2.6 % at 52,224 vs 61,696
equals the 4070's number; the larger P123 / P127 losses came from off arms with almost no host tail.

## Corrections later

## What it changed

- D26 design input: a late page-bound reserve (no line taken before sparse engages). This became D26h (late reserve +
  self-timing engage), checked here in P146.

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method: split a fixed cost into "engine overhead" and "line cost" with a pinned-line control arm
  (BONSAI_KV_VRAM_CELLS = the shorter line, feature off).
- Finding (Turing, PCIe 3.0): decode past the line has a step from "no host tail" to "any host tail", then a small
  slope; compare arms only at equal host-tail state.
