## Question

Is the T4 build the product when `LLAMA_FAST_READ` is not set, and how fast and how lossy is T4-A on the 2060 (sm_75)
at 245k with the round-2 setting `keep=16384,bmax=1`?

## Why

T4-A (events E71) reads only a kept set of tokens with the full model. A scoring pass over the first 8 of 64 blocks
picks them. The 4070 gave 7/24 (round 1) and 9/24 (round 2, `bmax=1`) at 245k. The 2060 numbers check the speed on
sm_75 and the loss on a second card, before the round-2 posts.

## Plan and gate

Script header (gates of #138 08:37Z, applied to the 2060): "T4-0 (must pass): det_answer at 4k and 32k, fresh server
each, product zip bins vs bin-t4b with LLAMA_FAST_READ unset: equal sha256 at both depths. T4-1: the first n24
request's wall time at 245k (cold, includes the scoring pass), vs the 2060 dense cold ~1,489 s (P151/16b). T4-2: needle
n24 at 245k with LLAMA_FAST_READ=keep=16384,bmax=1; dense ref on the 2060 = P148: 23/24 (miss vault 14). No pass / fail
for T4 itself (opt-in lossy mode); report speed and every lost item. 0 CUDA errors."

## Method

det_answer.py: one quick_tps "code" prompt at a depth, greedy, 400 tokens; it saves the answer bytes and prints their
sha256. needle_depth.py: 24 vault codes at even positions in ~245k tokens of filler, one question per vault; the first
request reads the whole prompt, so its wall time is the cold read.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Script bug (notebook 05:17 CDT): "the runner's PATH has no sha256sum, so the script's own compare was empty vs empty;
  the PASS rests on det_answer's printed hashes and a Python recheck of the 4 answer files." The run.txt lines "zip
  d4000 sha256 " have no value. The det_answer hashes in run.txt are equal at both depths.
- Found by this record writer: the notebook and the #138 post say "18 lost items say the text has no such code". The
  receipts show 15 such answers. Three answers (vaults 7, 11, 20) are cut at 40 characters before the content, so their
  content is not in the record. Vault 22 is the wrong code. 15 + 3 + 1 = 19 lost items.
- The run did not add the `keep=32768` arm. The 2060's post (11:51:29Z): "I will not run the keep 32k arm now."

## Verdict

T4-0 PASS: with `LLAMA_FAST_READ` unset, bin-t4b gives the product's answer bytes at 4k and 32k. With `keep=16384,bmax=1`
at 245k, T4-A answers in ~238 s per question (6.2x faster than a dense cold read) and gets 5/24 (dense 23/24), with one
wrong code. On this card the scoring pass is 76 % of the time (180.8 of ~238 s), so T4-A cannot be faster than ~8x here.
T4-A stays an off-by-default experiment.

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method: an identity gate for an opt-in mode compares the mode-off answer bytes with the product bins at two depths,
  in fresh servers. Compute the hashes in Python (the queue runner's PATH has no `sha256sum`).
- Data (8 GB, sm_75): T4-A scoring over 8 of 64 blocks at 245k costs 180.8 s; the rest of a question (scores, the kept read of 18,688 tokens, the answer) ~49-51 s
  (237.6-239.6 s minus the scoring pass).
- Pitfall: a 40-character answer record cannot tell a miss from a wrong answer. Keep enough text to grade the content.
