## Question

With the shipped setup (q3_0 K/V default, BONSAI_SPARSE=1 at FRAC 1/8), does sparse equal dense item by item at 245k?

## Why

The 8 GB card needs a quality receipt for the shipped q3_0 + sparse setup. P141 had checked the same sets with q4_0
on bundle-20261014.

## Plan and gate

Script header: "Gate (declared here, before the run): sparse = dense on every item (strict); misses listed per item
(file / fn / position); if a miss lies inside the VRAM line, it is reported as such (sparse cannot drop those cells)."

## Method

245k, a fresh server per test: code_needle --n 20 and needle_depth --n 24, dense then sparse.

## Results

| arm | copies (n 20) | needles (n 24) | misses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dense | 20/20 | 23/24 | vault 14, position 0.562 (want 8104, answer 1968) |
| sparse (1/8) | 20/20 | 23/24 | vault 14, position 0.562 (want 8104, answer 1968) |

The missed needle is at ~138k of 245k tokens (0.562 x 245,000), past both VRAM lines (80,128 dense; 69,120 sparse).
Dense misses it in the same way.

| gate | value | result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sparse = dense on every item | same 43 of 44 items right, same miss | PASS |

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- The result entry came ~4.5 h after the run (18:35 CDT): the queue ran P119, P120 and fillers after P144, and the
  4070's D26h posts were missed for ~3 h (events E49).

## Verdict

PASS. With the shipped q3_0 + 1/8 setup, sparse = dense at 245k on the 2060 set. The one miss is the model's.
