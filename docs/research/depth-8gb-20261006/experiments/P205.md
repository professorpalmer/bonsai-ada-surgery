## Verdict

On the 2060, GROW 4096 (P200) is the lever: it halves the restart turns. Save after the answer on top of it is safe (30/30
answers, normal turns -2.0 %, session -2.3 %), but a restart is still bounded by the re-read since the last save, so B
fails. Unlike the 4070 G1 run, C passes here: the next turn does not wait long for a ~4.5 s save.
