## Question

Does slot 3 keep the exact answers and stay cool over a long agent session with saves, restores and restarts?

## Why

P202 showed +5-6 % on cold reads and the exact det 4k output. A soak with many server starts, decode and slot files is
the next check before the slot goes into the serve notes as an option.

## Plan and gate

Notebook 08:43 CDT (declared before the run): "P200's 30-turn session (g8192) on slot 3, gate 30/30 answers = P200
stock, 0 CUDA errors, temp < 85 C."

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Found by this record writer: the script header still holds P200's G1 gate text ("with 4096, the mean restart-turn
  wall >= 25 % lower ..."). The gates that the summary prints are the notebook's (30/30, temp < 85 C).
- Found by this record writer: summary.txt has no temperature line. The inline parser read the 4-field mem.txt lines in
  the wrong way and stopped. The values above come from mem.txt.
- Found by this record writer: the line was 79,616-82,944 (other programs 391-435 MiB at the starts), against 86,016-86,528 in
  P200 (341-349 MiB). With fewer VRAM cells the deep turns are slower, so the -4.1 % is not on the same line as the
  reference. The answers were equal all the same.

## Verdict

PASS. Slot 3 keeps all 30 answers of the stock session, stays at 77 C or less and draws at most 214 W. It is a safe
optional tune on this card. It is not a launcher default, because other 2060s may not hold +100.
