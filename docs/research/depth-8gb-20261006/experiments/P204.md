## Question

On the real 8 GB card, does "save after the answer" (no prefill-only call; the save runs after the client has the
answer) make the restart turns of the P164 session faster than the save before the request?

## Why

On the 4070 the A/B (run 5) passed B (restart turns -56.1 %) but failed C (normal turns +94.2 %): the next turn waited
for the post-answer save. The release session asked for the same A/B on the 2060 "with the defaults (GROW 4096 on 8 GB)"
(14:11:33Z).

## Plan and gate

Script header (gates A-F, the release session's): "A answers equal 30/30; B restart turns 4-28 mean >= 30 % lower; C
normal turns mean at most 10 % higher; D arm wall time at most "before" + 5 %; E the prefix test passes in the layer
log, >= 1 save after the answer, saves at most "before" + 2; F 0 CUDA errors, no lc-saving-* files left." Before each
restart the job waits until `GET /layer/slot-cache` returns `"busy": false` (max 300 s).

- Saves (both arms) 3.89-7.58 s; restores 2.06-5.86 s; 4 evictions per arm.
- No "WARN layer still busy" line: every wait for idle ended within 300 s.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- My mistake: the layer runs outside the launcher. The launcher sets `BONSAI_SLOT_CACHE_GROW=4096` for its own process
  on 8 GB (#182), but `p204_layer.py` did not get it, and the script even ran `unset BONSAI_SLOT_CACHE_GROW`. The layer
  used its default 8192 in both arms (the layer logs show saves every ~12,000 tokens, as P200 g8192). So this is not the
  requested recipe. P205 repeats it with GROW 4096 exported.
- The request called this run "P201"; this record keeps its own number (P201 is the power test).

## Verdict

A valid after-vs-before A/B at GROW 8192: answers equal, normal turns and session within the limits, but the restart
turns gain only 6.2 % (B asks 30 %). On the 2060 a restart turn is mostly the re-read since the last save; saving after
the answer moves the save but not its spacing.

## Corrections later

- P205 (GROW 4096): the same picture, B -5.4 %.

## What it changed

- P205 was queued at once (10:51 CDT).
- Lesson (notebook 10:51 CDT): "an external layer runner must get the launcher's layer env (GROW, AFTER, SAVE_PATH)
  explicitly."

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Data (8 GB, 2060, GROW 8192): save after the answer: restart turns -6.2 %, normal +6.9 %, session -0.6 %, answers
  equal.
- Pitfall: a helper that runs part of the product outside the launcher (here the layer) does not get the launcher's
  per-card defaults; export them by hand and check the helper's log (save spacing) before the run counts.
