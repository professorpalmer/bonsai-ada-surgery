## Question

Does the P213 gain hold deeper, at 200k?

## Why

The mechanism predicts a smaller gain with depth: the extra 10.5k VRAM cells are a smaller part of the system-RAM tail.

## Plan and gate

Script header: "default sizing, replay off then on, one det_answer at 200k each (a cold read + 400 decoded tokens).
Reported: the VRAM line, decode tok/s at 200k, the prefill time (server log), min free VRAM, 0 CUDA errors / spill
lines."

## Results

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| det 200k (198,996 tokens) decode | 12.6 tok/s | **12.9 tok/s** | **+2.4 %** |
| prefill | 1,062,940.53 ms (187.21 tok/s) | 1,057,285.27 ms (188.21 tok/s) | +0.5 % |

- The host tail: 127,340 - 80,128 = 47,212 cells (off) vs 36,716 (on) at 128k: -22 %; at 198,996 tokens: 118,868 vs
  108,372: -9 %. The decode gain follows: +13.9 % at 128k (P213), +2.4 % at 200k.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- None. Each arm is one run; +2.4 % is near the run-to-run noise of a single det read.

## Verdict

Reported. Replay rollback is not slower at 200k (+2.4 % decode, prefill the same, +14 MiB headroom). The gain falls
with depth, as the cut of the host tail predicts.
