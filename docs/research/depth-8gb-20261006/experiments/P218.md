## Question

If only V keeps 2 bits and K stays at q4_0, does the 64k PPL stay within +1 % of q3_0?

## Why

P216: a full 2-bit KV fails (+1.83 %). The next step in the notebook (10:39 CDT) is V only, with K kept at q4_0. "K 3.5
+ V ~2.5 bits = ~-14 % KV bytes (~+15k VRAM cells at today's line)" (12:27 CDT; an estimate, not measured).

## Plan and gate

Script header (declared before the run): "Gate G1: v2 within +1.0 % of P216's q3_0 64k PPL (7.2098). A pass is worth
~-14 % KV bytes (K 3.5 + V ~2.5 bits) and would need a mixed K/V FA path."

## Results

| arm | 64k PPL (2 chunks) | vs q3_0 7.2098 | 16k PPL (18 chunks) | vs q3_0 7.9491 | G1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| v2 = V 2-bit, K q4_0 | **7.2491** +/- 0.07124 | **+0.55 %** | 8.0413 +/- 0.05534 | +1.16 % | **PASS** |
| v3 = V 3-bit, K q4_0 (control) | 7.2126 +/- 0.07119 | +0.04 % | 7.9039 +/- 0.05438 | -0.57 % | - |

- No CUDA error or "failed" line in the logs (other than the fit-params note).

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- The logs do not print the V-only state; the PPL values (v2 7.2491 vs P216 b2 7.3417) show that the switch acted.
- Engine note (commit 37a51194f): the switch picks the level count per set_rows call (V cache rows: the fake bits; K
  rows: 0) and sets the device symbol with `cudaMemcpyToSymbolAsync` on the op's stream, so the value is in order with the
  K and V writes that read it.

## Verdict

PASS on G1: V-only 2-bit costs +0.55 % PPL at 64k (+1.16 % at 16k). It goes to the retrieval gate G2 (P219) before any
engine work on a mixed K / V type.
