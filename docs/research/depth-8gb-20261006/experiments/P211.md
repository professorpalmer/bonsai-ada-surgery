## Question

Does 18f's layer (a save after every answer, as a delta; a restore that lands exactly on the last answer) work on the
2060, with the same answers as P205?

## Why

Not asked. 18f is the bundle Cary serves next; the 2060 has the 8 GB defaults.

## Plan and gate

Script header (declared before the run): "12 turns of the P164 session ... with the defaults (save after every answer),
GROW 4096 exported, restarts before turns 4 / 7 / 10: answers equal to P205's after=1 arm on 12/12; saves after the
answer are deltas; restores work; 0 CUDA errors."

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- The script header says "through the 18d zip's own launcher"; the script ran `release_1018f/x` (a copy-paste in the

## Verdict

Reported: 18f works on the 2060. Every answer saves a ~0.4 s delta, a restart restores to the last answer in 2.6-4.2
s, and restart turns that re-read in P205 do not re-read here. Two answers differ from P205 by whitespace; P212 shows
that P211's form is the cache-off form.
