## Question

DLLs) keep the 8 GB defaults and speed on the 2060?

## Why

Every bundle gets a post-zip check on this card. 0058 should not change decode, but three ggml DLLs were rebuilt.

## Plan and gate

Script header: "Runs while the D26h engine builds (CPU -j 4): a decode miss is re-run after the build before any
verdict. ... Gates (declared here, before the run): sha256 match; the zip's start-server.ps1 has no control bytes
(0x00-0x08, 0x0B, 0x0C, 0x0E-0x1F); --version commit c2cf6d6450; ADD, GET_ROWS, FLASH_ATTN_EXT 0 FAIL; default launch
