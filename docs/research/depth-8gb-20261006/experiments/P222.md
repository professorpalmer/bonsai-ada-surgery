## Question

Can replay with draft 1 below the VRAM line and draft 2 past it keep the deep gain of 2 / 2 and remove its cost at 4k
and 64k?

## Why

P221: the 2 / 2 default is -3.3 % at 4k and -3.7 % at 64k against 1 / 1, while 128k gains about +30 %. P215 showed that
the deep gain comes from SPEC_DEEP = 2, and P221 showed that the shallow cost comes with lower acceptance on prose and
bash.

## Plan and gate

Script header (declared before the run): "test replay + draft 1 below the VRAM line / 2 past it (BONSAI_SPEC=1,
BONSAI_SPEC_DEEP=2) on the same zip and launcher, quick_tps, a fresh server per depth and arm, in one block: d12 vs def
(2 / 2) vs old (BONSAI_RS_REPLAY=0, 1 / 1) at 4k, 64k and 128k. Gate (declared before the run): d12 within 1 % of old at
4k and 64k, and within 3 % of def at 128k -> propose d12 as the 8 GB default."

Order in the block, per depth: d12, def, old.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- One run per arm and depth (no repeat). In P221 the repeats agree within 0.1 tok/s.
- Found by this record writer: the #138 post gives the load as "server 7,488 MiB on load for both". The d12 server at 4k
  shows 7,486 MiB because its idle reading was 2 MiB higher (212 against 210 MiB); "used" is 7,698 MiB for every d12 and
  def server.
- The #138 post gives the replay step cost as "~2.2 % at 4k and ~1.6 % at 64k". Per prompt the values are 2.1-2.5 % and
  1.6-1.7 %.
- The option B comparison (d12 against the published 2 / 2) is not the declared gate. The post and the decision say so.
- Found by this record writer: no quality gate ran on d12 itself. P217 passed d11 (1 / 1 with replay) and d22 (2 / 2 with
  replay) on the P170 code set; d12 drafts as d11 below the line and as d22 past it.

## Verdict

FAIL by the declared gate: d12 is 2.4 % (4k) and 1.7 % (64k) slower than 1 / 1, over the 1 % limit. The 128k part
passes (-0.3 % against 2 / 2). Against the published 2 / 2 default, d12 is better or equal at every measured depth
(+1.0 %, +2.3 %, -0.3 %) at the same used VRAM, and its acceptance below the line equals 1 / 1.

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Data (8 GB, 2060, replay on): 1 / 2 = 52.4 / 39.9 / 29.6 tok/s at 4k / 64k / 128k (acceptance 0.784 / 0.778 / 0.692);
  2 / 2 = 51.9 / 39.0 / 29.7; 1 / 1 without replay = 53.7 / 40.6 / 22.4.
- Method: when a two-part change fails, split the parts (draft below the line, draft past the line, replay) in one block,
  per prompt type.
- Pitfall: a declared gate that fails stays failed. If the option is still better than the current default, state that as
  a new comparison and let the release owner choose.
