## Question

How fast is the PTQ1_0 prefill matmul alone on sm_75, and is the weights part of a cold read near what the card can do?

## Why

The cost model (events E78) puts 516 s of a 245k read (35 %) in the non-attention part, at about 25 TFLOP/s. If the
PTQ1_0 matmul alone is much faster than that, the gap is outside the matmul; if it is near, a faster matmul kernel is
the lead.

## Plan and gate

Script header: "test-backend-ops perf, MUL_MAT 4096 x 512 x 14336 (the generic perf case), for PTQ1_0 (MMQ int8 tile path
at every batch), Q8_0, Q4_0, F16 (cuBLAS), and PTQ1_0 with GGML_CUDA_PTQ1_0_MMQ_MAX_BATCH=256 (the cuBLAS fp16 fallback
at n = 512). Bins: bundle-20261017 zip. No server runs. Reported: GFLOPS per type; nothing ships from this."

table / kernel is a kernel-session lead (each +10 % on the matmul = ~-46 s at 245k, ~-12 s at 64k; it stacks with T5)."

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- run.txt holds only the "CUDA graph warmup reset" lines of the first grep (the numbers print on later lines); the
  numbers above are from perf_all.txt.
- The cuBLAS fallback is +12 % on this case (31.8 vs 28.3). The notebook: "the fork keeps MMQ for accuracy". No
  accuracy test ran here.
- Not confirmed from the receipts: the Turing int8 peak behind "~25 %" (no peak figure is in the receipts or the entry);
  the "~22 %" of the research entry is the earlier estimate.
- The kernel facts (the Ampere MMQ table on Turing, the m8n8k16 emulation) are from the notebook entry; this run does not
  measure them.

## Verdict

Reported. The PTQ1_0 MMQ matmul runs at 28.3 TFLOPS on the 2060, about the same as q8_0 / q4_0 MMQ and the whole-model
weights part (~25.5 TFLOP/s). The weights part of a cold read is the matmul. A faster Turing int8 tile path is a
kernel-session lead; it stacks with any attention saving (T5).
