## Question

bring the copies back to 10/10?

## Why

f16 bounds; dense was 10/10. P105 on the 2060 (d6c6e9f01) had missed another item (_strptime.py
_calc_julian_from_U_or_W, position 0.554). PR #56 (patch 0055) and #49 (launcher) waited for this answer.
