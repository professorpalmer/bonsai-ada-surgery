## Question

Do the zip's own bins (a fat build, not byte-equal to bin-rc55) keep the RC's sparse speed, headroom and quality, and
the HumanEval score?

## Why

P135 and P136 ran on the rig's 75-real build of the RC. The zip is a fat sm 75/86/89 build (P138 hashes differ).

## Plan and gate

Script header: "Gates (declared here, before the run): 245k sparse >= 1.8x dense; trace min free >= 60 MiB in both arms;
sparse 245k copies 10/10 and needles 12/12 (= dense P136); HumanEval >= 157/164; 0 one-token."

## Method

(1) 245k cold, dense then BONSAI_SPARSE=1, one server each, 10 s trace. (2) 245k code_needle --n 10 and needle_depth
--n 12 with BONSAI_SPARSE=1, a fresh server per test. (3) HumanEval 164 on the zip bins, default switches (medium
thinking, temp 0, 20,480 cap).
