## Verdict

The declared gates pass: +8.3 % at 64k, exact det 4k, 80 C. But the long read falls behind past ~125k and ends 37 %
slower than the stock references, with the GPU waiting (low power, "GPU idle" reason at 100 % util). Not attributable
from this run: P208 repeats the long read with stock in the same block and the CPU load logged. Slot 3 stays the tested
option.
