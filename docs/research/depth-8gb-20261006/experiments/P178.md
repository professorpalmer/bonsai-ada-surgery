## Question

With the skip on 256-cell blocks merged into runs (so the kept cells are the sink run and the window run, not whole 32k
chunks), how fast is the T5 cold read at 245k and 128k? And does a 32k window, or 4 dense full-attention layers, bring
the prose set back?

## Why

P175: the fixed 32k chunk grain keeps too much (1.10x at 128k). P174b: the 16k window loses 6 prose items. Two knobs:
a wider window, and some full-attention layers on a second, unwindowed mask (`dense=il:il:...`, fd17c67db: "keeps the
listed attention layers on a second, unwindowed mask (the same packed mask, filled with the window off)").
