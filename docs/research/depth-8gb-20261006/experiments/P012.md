## Question

Can the MTP head be made cheap enough in VRAM (Q4_0 head weights, a shared CUDA pool, a smaller draft ubatch) that a
drafted mode beats the no-head mode on this card?

## Why

P005: the head cost ~600 MiB over budget (C at 7,255 MiB, 144 MiB free) but decoded 18-25% faster in VRAM. The P5
result named the next ports: requant (-204 MiB) and shared pool (-188 MiB), both numbers of the source fork.

## Plan and gate

"Head file: mtp-procreations with blk.64.* requantized Q8_0 -> Q4_0 (mirai-s-ada tooling/requant_mtp.py; proof: strip
the head, sha256 == official PTQ1_0)."
"Step 1, VRAM at N=16384 (q4_0, draft 1, tail 2, window 16384), server VRAM at load + 4k probe + acceptance:
C0 q8 head, pool off | C1 q4 head, pool off | C2 q4 head, pool on + DRAFT_UBATCH=256."
"Step 2: size N for the cheapest passing variant to P11's passing free margin; depth sweep 4k/16k/32k/64k vs B at P11's
N." Gates written before the run (quoted):
- "each saving within 30% of the source fork's number (head -204 MiB, pool -188 MiB) or reported as not transferring;"
- "acceptance with the q4 head within 2 pp of the q8 head (source fork: 77.7 vs 78.2%);"
- "decision vs B: P5's rule (best at 32k unless >25% at both 4k and 16k), min free >= P11's passing margin;"
- "drafting differs from non-drafting at rounding level on every card (documented) - not a gate; KL of the drafted
  default vs B is reported if the head mode becomes the default."

## Method

Prep (after P10b): build bin-p12 and requantize the head in parallel; strip the q4-head file with the graft tool's
merge.py --strip and compare sha256 with the base file. Step 1: per arm, serve, server VRAM = used - idle, memclock_probe
4k (2 reps), acceptance = mean of "draft acceptance" lines in the server log. Step 2: N for C2 solved for ~257 MiB free
(`n = ((8007 - 257 - idle) MiB - fixed - 131072 x 1152) / 17280`), then quick_tps 4k/16k/32k/64k for C2 and for B at
N=77568 on the same binary.

Decision (P5 rule, best at 32k): C2. "Default for 8 GB with display on the card becomes: q4_0, q4-head MTP (draft 1,
tail 2), shared pool, ~38.4k in VRAM. B (no head, ~77.5k in VRAM) is the long-context mode (>~40k)."
"Not measured: KL of drafted vs undrafted output (drafting differs at rounding level on every card, documented)."
"Anomaly: prefill B 237 / 218 / 185 / 147 tok/s vs C2 405 / 341 / 264 / 177 at the same depths; B at N=38656 (P5)
prefilled 356-397 at 4k. Needs a controlled A/B (P14) before 77,568 ships anywhere."

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- The plan named KL of the drafted default vs B "if the head mode becomes the default". It became the default; the KL
  was not measured (stated in the result). Task quality was measured later (P031: HumanEval 157/164, outside this range).

## Verdict

"Cheap head + 38,400 positions beats B up to 32k; B keeps 64k; prefill anomaly on B at 77,568."

## Corrections later

- P015: tail 1 frees one 152 MiB GDN plane; the drafted default moved to tail 1 at N=47,360.
- P013/P018: C2 (tail 2) gave one-token answers at 32k after a prompt-cache restore in some runs (2 of 6 runs).
- P064 (2026-10-08, outside this range): "Patch 0037's GGML_CUDA_SHARED_POOL, set by the 8 GB preset (shipped in PR #6 /
  bundle-20261007-8gb), breaks fresh long prompts with MTP drafting on this card." Hotfix PR #10 removed it from the
  preset. P083 (2026-10-09): "P12 (old engine): pool + DRAFT_UBATCH 256 saved 62 MiB here, then caused the 1-token race
  (fixed by 0045, in bin-0046)." With patch 0045 the pool saved ~51 MiB and passed.
- P054 (2026-10-09): "The P18 failures (2026-10-07) ran with GGML_CUDA_SHARED_POOL=1 (bin-p12 C2 config). Most likely
  the same pool race."
- P012's prefill anomaly on B was a memory-pressure cliff (P014/P014b), not deterministic (P014c).

## What it changed

- The Q4_0 head (212 MB smaller, -214 MiB VRAM) became part of the 8 GB drafting mode and the repo tooling
  (build/make_mtp_q4head.ps1, verified byte-identical in P020).
- The shared pool + DRAFT_UBATCH 256 went into the release candidates (bin-rc, bin-rc2, bundle-20261007) and was later
  removed by the hotfix (P064) until patch 0045.

## Toolbox

- Method: requantize only the MTP head block (blk.64.*) Q8_0 -> Q4_0; prove the trunk is untouched by stripping the head
  and comparing sha256 with the official file.
- Method: measure each claimed VRAM saving on your card against the source's number (within 30% or "does not transfer").
- Finding: the q4 head costs 2.7 pp of acceptance and ~1% decode here; the VRAM it frees is worth ~12k q4_0 positions.
- Lesson: a VRAM saving that shares memory between contexts on different streams (shared pool) needs a correctness test
  at depth (fresh long prompts, prompt-cache restore), not only a speed test.
- Model-specific: Bonsai's MTP head is one extra block (blk.64) grafted onto the 64-block trunk.
