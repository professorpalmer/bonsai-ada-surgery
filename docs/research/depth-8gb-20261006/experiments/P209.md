## Question

Does bundle-20261018c keep the 18b output and speed on the 2060, and do its delta saves and chain restores work in the
P164 session with the 8 GB defaults?

## Why

18c saves only the cells since the last save of a conversation (`pos_min` on save, `append` on restore); a full save
comes every 8 deltas. On the 4070 dev drive a full save of a 173k state took 70.6 s, a delta 0.08 s. The release
session asked for a 2060 check.

## Plan and gate

Script header (declared before the run): "(1) det_answer 4k: zip 18b then zip 18c, in one block: equal sha. (2) a cold
64k read on 18c (stock 306.5-307.7 tok/s). (3) 12 turns of P164's session through the 18c zip's own launcher + its layer
(save after the answer and delta saves on by default; GROW 4096 exported because the layer runs outside the launcher),
restarts before turns 4, 7, 10: answers equal to P205's after=1 arm (the same defaults minus deltas) on 12/12; the layer
log says "delta saves on"; the slot folder holds small delta files; restores work after each restart; 0 CUDA errors."

## Verdict

PASS. 18c keeps the 18b output and the stock 64k speed. Delta saves take ~0.5 s (P205's full saves ~4.5 s) with ~271 MB
files that hold the ~149 MiB recurrent state, and chain restores work. On this card the saves were already fast, so the
gain is small, but nothing regresses.
