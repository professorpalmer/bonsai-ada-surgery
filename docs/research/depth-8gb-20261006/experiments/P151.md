## Question

In a cold 245k prefill on 8 GB, what share of the time is attention (FLASH_ATTN_EXT) and what share is the fixed work
(MUL_MAT ptq1_0, GATED_DELTA_NET, rest)?

## Why

Cary (2026-10-16): "28 min at max context is unusable". The size of each lever on #138 (block-sparse prefill / FA
kernel vs prefill matmul) depends on these shares.

## Plan and gate

Script header: "Reading (declared before the run): FLASH_ATTN_EXT share per table vs depth; MUL_MAT ptq1_0 +
GATED_DELTA_NET + rest. This sizes the levers: block-sparse prefill / FA kernel (attention part) vs prefill matmul
(fixed part). Also the wall ttft vs P143 (1,695 s)."

## Method

One fresh server, one cold 245k code prompt (quick_tps --depth 245000). The engine prints an op table every 100
graphs (~512-token ubatches), so the tables show the op shares at ~50k, ~100k, ... ~245k of depth. Graphs are off
inside the timer: absolute ms are inflated; the shares are the data.

## Results

245k code: prefill 143.7 tok/s, ttft 1,697.34 s (evaluated 243,896), decode 8.5 tok/s (mean 8.3 with op timing on).

attention)."

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- None. Table 7 reads 1069.2 ms/graph, lower than tables 4-6; the receipts do not show why (not analysed for this
  record).

## Verdict

DONE. At 245k cold, attention is about two thirds of the prefill time; the fixed part (mostly MUL_MAT) is about a
third and does not grow per table. With free attention a cold 245k read would still take ~10 min on this card.
