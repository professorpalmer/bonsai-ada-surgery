## Question

Does a 2-bit KV keep quality once K / V are rotated (attn-rot) and the 4 levels are placed by Lloyd-Max?

## Why

Research pass 2 (notebook 08:46 CDT), nugget 2: "KV below 3 bits (TurboQuant 2.5-bit after rotation; KIVI): ~127k VRAM
cells, est. +20-35 % decode at 200-245k; lossy". P100 (2026-10-10) measured 2-bit at +1.86 % (64k), and its record says
it ran with no rotation. The cheap probe first: the fake-bits hook keeps only 2^b levels of the q4_0 grid in set_rows, so
quality can be measured before a real type is built.

## Plan and gate

Script header (declared before the run): "llama-perplexity on wikitext-2 test, as P100: a 16k window (all chunks) and a
64k window (2 chunks), the same pinned KV line in every arm. ... Gate G1 (declared before the run, from the research
note): a 2-bit arm passes if its 64k PPL is within +1.0 % of q3 (and report 16k). A pass sends that arm to G2 (needles +
copies at 160k, item by item vs q3) in a later run."

## Results

| arm | 64k PPL (2 chunks) | vs q3_0 | 16k PPL (18 chunks) | vs q3_0 | G1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| q3 = real q3_0 (shipped) | 7.2098 +/- 0.07105 | - | 7.9491 +/- 0.05477 | - | - |
| b0 = real q4_0 | 7.2074 +/- 0.07120 | -0.03 % | 7.9050 +/- 0.05444 | -0.55 % | - |
| b2 = 2-bit uniform, rotation on | **7.3417** +/- 0.07202 | **+1.83 %** | 8.1931 +/- 0.05655 | +3.07 % | FAIL |
| b2nr = 2-bit uniform, rotation off | 7.6116 +/- 0.07491 | +5.57 % | 8.5215 +/- 0.05904 | +7.20 % | FAIL |
| b20 = 2-bit Lloyd-Max, rotation on | 7.6819 +/- 0.07669 | +6.55 % | 8.9126 +/- 0.06351 | +12.12 % | FAIL |

- Rotation matters: 2-bit uniform goes from +5.57 % (off) to +1.83 % (on) at 64k.
- Lloyd-Max levels (no zero level) are worse than the uniform grid here.
- q3_0 and q4_0 equal P108's values (7.9491 / 7.2098 and 7.9050 / 7.2074).
- b2 equals P100's 2-bit result to four decimals at both windows (P100: 8.1931 at 16k, 7.3417 at 64k).

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Found by this record writer: the summary's "rot" field is empty in every line: the grep for "attn_rot_k" found no line
  in the logs, so the receipts do not show the rotation state. The PPL values show that `LLAMA_ATTN_ROT_DISABLE=1`
  changed the result.
- The script header calls b2nr "P100's setting". The equal values say the opposite: P100 matches b2 (rotation on). See
  Corrections later in P100.

## Verdict

FAIL. A full 2-bit KV misses G1 at both windows, also in its best form (uniform levels with rotation: +1.83 % at 64k,
+3.07 % at 16k). Full 2-bit KV is closed. The next probe is V-only 2-bit (P218).
