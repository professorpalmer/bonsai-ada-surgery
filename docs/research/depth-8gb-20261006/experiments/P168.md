## Question

Does the v5 guard stay silent for an hour of normal use with the display and a playing browser on the same 8 GB card,
and is it still alive at the end (one small hog -> the line)?

## Why

The 2060 drives the desktop. A browser and a video change other programs' GPU memory all the time. A guard that prints
false lines, or that dies after an invalid counter sample (the PR #164 review), is not usable on this card. v5: "Each
pass has its own try, and only valid samples count. Rule (2) now needs two samples in a row, and its base no longer
follows a program that shrinks during a squeeze. The guard attaches only to the server this launcher started."

## Plan and gate

Script header (gates declared on #138 08:50:36Z): "A browser plays a video on the same card for the hour ... Reads: a
cold 128k at the start, a cold 64k at ~15 / 30 / 45 min (a unique first line each, so no prompt-cache reuse), 3 short
requests after each 64k; then a (free - 100) MiB torch ballast for 45 s. PASS: 0 spill lines during the hour; read
speeds within 2 % of P167b g4a (529.21 s at 128k) / the usual 64k (P163 302.6 tok/s); the "other programs took" line
after the ballast. Report min free VRAM and CUDA errors."

## Method

p168_read.py: one request per read with a unique first line ("Session <salt> at <time>.") + quick_tps filler +
"Summarize the notes in one line."; `max_tokens` 64; it prints the server's prefill tok/s and the wall time. Short
requests: "Say hi.", `max_tokens` 32. A 10 s nvidia-smi trace of free VRAM and GPU utilization.

## Deviations, incidents, void runs

- Found by this record writer: the 64k gate "within 2 %" is over the band on the fast side (+2.2 % to +2.3 %). The
  reference is P163's sweep (60,477 evaluated tokens, quick_tps code prompt, line 80,640); P168's reads are 64,017
  tokens of p168_read.py filler with the line at 86,784. The notebook and the post read it as a pass (not slower). The
  same kind of case as the P150 160k wording (E57).
- Found by this record writer: the trace cannot show the browser's GPU load. Between the reads, utilization.gpu reads
  0 % in every sample (04:15-04:26: 63 of 63; 04:45-04:56: 63 of 63; 242 of 354 samples in all). The video decoder is
  not in that counter, and Edge may throttle a window that is not in front. The #138 post says "GPU utilization 0-100 %
  in the trace"; the 100 % samples are the server's reads. So the hour shows 0 false lines with the display and a
  playing browser open; it does not show a measured browser load.
- The end ballast was 18 MiB (free 118 - 100). The guard said "191 MiB". The #138 post reads it as the other programs'
  committed memory that grew by ~170 MiB during the hour (the browser) without a change in free VRAM, "that fits the
  Total Committed basis". Not measured separately.
- Not confirmed from the receipts: the #138 post says "free was ~148 MiB at the first /health (the browser was up) ...
  so rule (2)'s limit was ~89 MiB". The trace reads 300 MiB at 03:56:06, after the load and before the 128k read, and
  148 MiB only from 03:57:41. The guard's base is not in the launcher output, so the record cannot say which free value
  the guard took as its base.
- The clip.webm video (5,293,251 B) is not in scripts/. No script for the clip is on the rig: it was made with an
  inline OpenCV command.

## Verdict

PASS. The v5 guard printed 0 lines in 1 hour with the display and a playing browser on the 2060, and it was still alive
at the end (one line within 46 s of an 18 MiB hog). The reads did not slow: 128k 239.2 tok/s (-0.6 % vs P167b), 64k 309.3-
309.6 tok/s. The line 86,784 is the launcher's normal answer to ~90 MiB more free VRAM at start, not the guard.

## Toolbox (for the next model)

- Method (display-load soak): a browser with its own profile plays a local video + a WebGL page on the same card for
  an hour; cold reads at fixed times with a unique first line; a small hog at the end proves the guard is still alive;
  close only the test profile.
- Pitfall: nvidia-smi utilization.gpu does not show video decode; log the decoder counter (or the browser's GPU
  process memory) if the load must be measured.
- Data (8 GB): the VRAM line moves about 80k cells per GiB of free VRAM at start; ~90 MiB less used by other programs
  moved it from 80,640 to 87,296.
